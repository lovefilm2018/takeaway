# Takeaway — Independent Restaurant Online Ordering Platform

> A direct-to-consumer online takeaway ordering and kitchen management platform built for independent takeaways and restaurants, offering a low-commission alternative (~5%) to high-fee delivery aggregators.

## 🍽️ Key Features

- **Customer Ordering Web App:** High-performance, mobile-first PWA for browsing menus, customizing dishes, selecting collection or delivery, and checking out seamlessly with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cards.
- **Kitchen & Staff Portal:** Tablet-optimized dashboard for live order acceptance, persistent audio alerts, quick prep time estimations (e.g. 20m, 40m), kitchen ticket progression, and instant out-of-stock toggles.
- **Fair Pricing Disruption:** Built to eliminate the 15%–30%+ fees charged by Just Eat / Deliveroo, charging only ~5% while giving restaurants direct ownership of their brand and customer relationships.
- **Pilot Deployment:** First implementation designed specifically for a local independent Chinese takeaway without existing online ordering.
